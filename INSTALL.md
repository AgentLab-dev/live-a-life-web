# Install Live a Life

Live a Life is a free kids' town game. There is no App Store and no account. Open this link, then add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:

**https://agentlab-dev.github.io/live-a-life-web/**

You can also scan this QR code. It points at the same link:

![QR code for Live a Life](./qr-play.png)

After the first visit, the game works with the wifi off. When a new version is ready, the title screen refreshes once. If a child is already playing, a Refresh button appears instead.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open the link in **Safari** (the compass icon). Other iPhone browsers cannot add the real home-screen app.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. A house icon named Live a Life appears with your other apps.
5. Open it from that icon. It fills the screen. The walking buttons stay clear of the notch and the home bar.

The page also shows a small card with the same steps. Tap **Not now** if you want it to stay quiet on this phone.

If you do not see Add to Home Screen, scroll down in the share sheet, or leave an in-app browser and use Safari.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app**, or open the browser menu and tap **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open **Live a Life** from the new icon.

## Computer

1. Open the link in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
2. Use the install icon in the address bar, or the browser menu, and choose **Install**.
3. You can also keep the tab open. Arrow keys or WASD walk around town.

## Play

Tap **Play**. Tap the ground to walk. On a phone, use the pad in the corner. Nothing here asks for a login, a chat, or a purchase.
